# Scientific Boundaries

This course teaches reproducible EEG engineering and sensor-level analysis. It is not a clinical system, a validated cognitive assessment, or evidence that a laboratory demonstration generalizes to a population.

## Claims supported by the workflow

When the relevant checks pass, the materials can support statements about:

- recording integrity, packet shape, malformed rows, decoded samples, and timing continuity;
- documented signal-quality flags and retained data;
- sensor-level time series, Welch PSD, band power, posterior Alpha dynamics, and Alpha peak frequency;
- within-recording or within-session condition contrasts;
- deterministic simulation behavior and saved-recording replay;
- a transparent spectral SSVEP decision rule on synthetic trials; and
- sensor-level magnitude-squared coherence in a named frequency band.

Every result should identify the source mode, sample rate, channel order, preprocessing, QC method, accepted duration or trials, parameters, software version, and random seed where relevant.

## Claims not supported by this course

Do not use these materials to claim:

- medical diagnosis, treatment response, or clinical decision-making;
- emotion, deception, personality, mental-health status, fatigue, attention, or an absolute cognitive-load score;
- an uncalibrated comparison between people or sessions;
- cortical source localization from the seven-channel montage;
- direct neural communication, causal connectivity, or a brain network from sensor coherence;
- population-level effects from one person, one session, or a synthetic example;
- deployment readiness or live performance from replay tests; or
- precise event-related latency without an independently measured display and marker path.

Use `Recording A` and `Recording B` when condition metadata are unknown. Terms such as “before treatment” and “after treatment” require a documented protocol and still do not establish treatment efficacy.

## Synthetic reference data

All recordings under `data/synthetic/` were generated by code. They use a NeuraDock-compatible text layout and a microvolt scale so the parser and analysis path can be tested, but they are not human EEG.

The synthetic eyes-closed condition intentionally contains a much stronger 10 Hz posterior rhythm than the eyes-open condition. The SSVEP trials are deliberately easy and yield 100% accuracy with the fixed spectral rule. These outcomes verify recovery of known injected structure. They are not benchmarks for a participant or device.

Every figure or table derived from these files should use the word **simulated** or **synthetic** near the result.

## Signal quality comes first

Put a quality warning near the beginning of any report when data are poor. State:

- malformed-row ratio;
- retained segments, duration, or trials for each condition;
- candidate bad channels;
- thresholds and preprocessing stage; and
- which observations remain visible despite the warning.

Do not interpolate across missing or rejected data to make an effect look continuous. Check electrode contact, headset placement, movement, jaw or neck tension, cable stability, electrical sources, and protocol timing before repeating an acquisition.

The checked-in thresholds are workflow heuristics. They help prioritize inspection; they do not determine the physiological or mechanical cause of an artifact.

## Alpha interpretation

The course defines Alpha as 8–13 Hz and emphasizes posterior channels. Report absolute or relative power, the frequency grid, and accepted duration. Label findings as sensor-level and session-relative.

Many people show higher posterior Alpha during eyes-closed rest than during eyes-open fixation. An individual session can show a weak, absent, or reversed contrast because of physiology, behavior, environment, placement, or data quality. Phrase a result as an observation in the recording, not proof of relaxation, attention, workload, or health.

Do not classify Alpha as weak, normal, or strong using a universal threshold. Such labels require a defined within-recording or calibrated-session reference.

## SSVEP interpretation and visual safety

The elective uses simulated 8, 12, and 15 Hz trials and a fixed spectral decision rule. No model is fitted. Its confusion matrix is a deterministic software check.

A future learned classifier must keep participant/session calibration separate from evaluation and split data by trial or block rather than correlated windows. Report balanced metrics, trial counts, uncertainty where feasible, and a no-control state.

Live visual stimulation requires a measured display refresh rate, frequencies realizable by the actual frame sequence, frame-drop logging, a visible photosensitivity warning, informed voluntary participation, conservative luminance and contrast, and an immediate stop control. Stop whenever the participant or operator asks.

## Coherence interpretation

Magnitude-squared coherence describes a frequency-specific association between two sensor signals. Common reference, volume conduction, shared artifacts, filtering, and limited data can all increase it. A high cell in a heatmap does not identify a direct anatomical connection or causal interaction.

Call the result **sensor-level Alpha-band coherence**, report the estimator and segment length, average only the 8–13 Hz bins, and discuss common-source limitations. Do not label the matrix a functional-connectivity network.

## Privacy and data handling

Process raw EEG locally by default. Use pseudonymous participant codes rather than names in filenames. Store consent records and identity keys separately. Define who can access the data, where it is retained, and when it is deleted.

Do not commit new participant EEG to Git without documented consent, de-identification, and redistribution permission. The two recordings in `data/teaching/lesson-02/` are the specific owner-approved public teaching release; their approval does not extend to other captures.

## Live-device status

The package includes a configurable TCP source, but the committed reference build has not measured a live NeuraDock device. Connection success alone does not establish signal quality. Mark live operation as verified only after the target setup receives valid samples and reports endpoint, duration, sample count, transport, malformed rows, sample-rate evidence, and QC.
